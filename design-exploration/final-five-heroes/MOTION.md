# Motion direction for the five final hero concepts

These are proposed animation specifications for a later build. The PNGs show static resting keyframes, not running animations. Identity text, navigation, the experience line, and key actions should remain readable while the visual layers move.

| Concept | Entrance | Continuous motion | Pointer response | Transition into work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forest / Sculpted Identity | Stepped portrait planes reveal in sequence while the name and role stay legible. | A very slow drift of the single matte backing form and a travelling accent along the header rule. | Small differential depth between portrait, backing plane and headline. | The portrait mask opens and the display type moves aside to reveal selected work. |
| Cobalt / Kinetic Architecture | Large type reveals along a diagonal; the portrait and cobalt plane settle into place. | A slow, shallow rotation of the folded plane changes its shadow. | The plane and portrait respond with restrained parallax. | The diagonal plane becomes a transition into the work section. |
| Ember / Signature in Motion | Name lettering reveals across the photographic depth layers. | A subtle orange light field evolves while the single proof teaser drifts slightly. | The portrait and proof teaser have different shallow depth responses. | The proof teaser expands into the OpsPilot case study. |
| Pace / Personal Authority | The personal name reveals behind the portrait in two complementary movements. | A single cobalt sculptural mark rotates slowly with subtle lighting. | Small shadow and depth changes give the portrait/name composition a tactile feel. | The large name contracts into the navigation as selected work enters view. |
| Alloy / Intelligence Studio | The monogram turns into its opening position while identity text appears first. | A slow sculptural rotation and restrained reflections give the scene continuous motion. | A small viewing-angle change reveals the monogram's construction. | The sculpture separates into planes that introduce the selected work. |

## Timing and restraint

- Show the name, role, and main action immediately; let the dimensional entrance resolve around them in roughly 0.8–1.2 seconds.
- Use a slow 10–16 second cycle for sculptural idle movement. Light and shadow can carry much of the motion.
- Keep pointer-based movement shallow: the portrait should feel anchored and the text should remain stable.
- Use one coordinated transition into the project section.
- A reduced-motion view can use the completed resting composition and simple opacity transitions.

The durations are design proposals, not measured performance claims. No animation has been implemented in this design round.
