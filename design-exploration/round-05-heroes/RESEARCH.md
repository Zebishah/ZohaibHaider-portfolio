# Research — hero design and motion

Researched 6 November 2026. The user's latest distinction is important: earlier project sections were partly successful, while the landing hero was not. This exploration therefore changes the opening composition and motion identity while retaining a visible route into OpsPilot, HR Intelligence, DSP, and RTA. No portrait is used.

## Research method

I searched portfolio case studies and creative-web motion collections, opened original sites, and captured desktop views of nine live-site targets: five personal portfolios and four studio sites. Miranda's portfolio was additionally read through its public page content. The studio sites are references for motion and composition, not claims about an individual engineer's portfolio.

The browser inspection reached usable content for Corentin Bernadou, Jonas Reymondin, Juan Mora Romero, Aristide Benoist, Kenta Toshikura, and monopo London. Lusion initially remained on its loader, Unseen initially remained at its entry screen, and Active Theory returned an unsupported-browser view. Those are recorded as partial inspections, not completed interactive reviews. Some Codrops article pages returned HTTP 403; for those, accessible author pages, indexed article summaries, and the original portfolios supplied the documented details. No claim is made to have reviewed every site on the internet.

## Design conclusions — my assessment

1. **Keep the identity clear.** The visitor should immediately find Zohaib's name and full-stack AI engineering role.
2. **Choose a principal motion behavior.** Flow, cloth, kinetic type, assembly, or depth should establish the hero's character. Other motion can support it.
3. **Give the motion a path into the content.** Let the selected visual system introduce the existing project exhibits or project index.
4. **Keep the project evidence.** AI and payment capability should be supported by OpsPilot, HR Intelligence, DSP, and the confirmed details of RTA.
5. **Judge type at rest.** Essential copy needs a readable resting state even when other layers continue moving.
6. **Choose a restrained palette.** A small coordinated set of colors should provide foreground contrast and guide attention.

These are design judgments drawn from the references, not objective rankings or award claims.
